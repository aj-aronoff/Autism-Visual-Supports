THE ONE YOU WERE ABOUT TO RESEARCH, WHICH IS A LOOK AND NOT A QUESTION
================================================================================

Roles, to log in to the Camera Sample App clone.

This is a look, not a question for OutSystems, and it is almost certainly one of
two things:

  1. The app defines an end-user role that your user has not been granted. In
     Studio the app's roles are visible among its elements; in the Portal, roles
     are granted to users in the user management area. You are an Administrator
     of the organisation, which is NOT the same as holding an app's end-user
     role - that distinction is the usual cause of this exact confusion.

  2. The sample app expects the built-in Users application, or a default
     administrator account that a clone did not carry with it.

Worth five minutes before it becomes a question. If it turns into a real
platform limitation rather than a grant you can make yourself, it earns a place
in group 2 - but do not spend a Slack question on a role assignment.
