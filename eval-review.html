Eight cases, two boards each

For each case, pick the board you would actually put on a fridge, then say why. The one you pick becomes the reference answer for that case, and the set becomes how we tell whether a change to the Composer prompt made things better or only different.

The comment matters more than the pick. The pick gives us a gold answer for one case. The reason generalises into a rule, and rules are what the prompt is made of. Two of these are deliberately close calls, one exposes a gap in the spec, and the last one has no board in it at all.

Tests: The hardest abstraction, and a gap in the spec. Role Choice exists in the data model but no support type uses it. Picking B says the spec needs fixing.

Rosa10 yearsIconsReaderHomeInterests: Weather and forecastsSensory: Dislikes sudden loud noiseTriggers: Plans changing after she has been told they are happening

Two cards: the change, then one pre-agreed replacement decided in advance on a calm day. Bets that a known substitute beats a choice made while upset.

What the parent says

Before

During

OnCompletion

Plan

  • PlacementBoth cards live on the kitchen calendar. The backup plan is written on the back of the second card, chosen by Rosa on a calm weekday.
  • ReinforcementNo reward. Naming the change out loud is the whole intervention. Deliver the backup plan the same day it is shown.

  2. Verbal — Say "Plan changed. Backup plan."
